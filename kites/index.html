Vigil

Keep the lanterns lit until dawn.

  • Drag a charm onto its lantern and hold to turn it over. Let go early and nothing happens.
  • A two-colour charm must turn both its lanterns, and once the first is turned nothing else can be played until the second is.
  • At windier difficulty levels the challenge is higher, and temporary obstacle cards can appear:
    Gust — every lantern burns down 25%
    Weary — slower movement
    Dazzled — blinded by the lights!
  • Drag a flask onto a matching lantern and hold to pour. Lift to keep the rest for later.
  • A flask is spent only when it's empty. Pouring into a full lantern spills — but still takes time. A two-colour flask has two sealed halves: each pours only into its own lantern.
  • Pouring into a dark lantern lights it. So does the lamplighter, on his own schedule. Lanterns burn at different speeds — the small ones go fast.
  • Above Calm, the crate hides Gust, Weary and Dazzled cards, the crate is bigger, and the oil gets thicker — slower to pour.
